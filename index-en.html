Scia

Scia is a photo journal for iPhone. Every day you save a memory lights up a star in a real constellation: day after day the figure fills in, and then a new one begins.

How it works

  • One memory a day. A photo, a title, an emotion and, if you like, a few lines. You can save as many memories as you want; a star lights up only with a memory from that same day.
  • Real constellations. The figures use the positions, brightness and colours of real stars. There are no deadlines: a figure stays in progress until it is complete.
  • Legends. Every constellation has a story in three chapters, unlocked as the figure lights up.
  • The monthly letter. At the end of each month Scia writes a letter about your memories. The text is generated on device with Apple Intelligence, where available.
  • Time capsules. A memory that opens on a future date.
  • Albums and shared albums. Albums collect memories with a constellation of their own. A shared album lights up together with up to three invited people.
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts. See your sky on the Home Screen and add a memory in a moment.

Free and Scia Stellare

The free plan includes unlimited memories, the small constellations, the monthly sky, the first chapter of every legend, the short letter and the widgets. Scia Stellare, an optional subscription managed by Apple, adds albums, shared albums, large figures, all legend chapters, the full letter, the year in review and themes.

Your data

Scia has no servers, shows no ads and does not track you. Your memories stay on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your iCloud. All details are in the privacy policy.
