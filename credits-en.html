Scia's constellations use public astronomical data. This page lists the sources and their licenses.

Stars

HYG Database v4.4 by David Nash (astronexus.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Star positions, brightness, colors, distances and names. HYG combines the Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogs.

Changes: Scia keeps the naked-eye stars, projects them for each figure (stereographic projection, north up) and fits the coordinates into a square. The derived data is shared under the same license: constellations.json (it can also be exported from the app, in Scia › Acknowledgements).
