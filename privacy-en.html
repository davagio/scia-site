Scia – Privacy Policy

In short

Scia shows no ads and uses no tracking tools. We don't collect personal data: your memories stay on your device and, if you turn it on, in your iCloud. We have no servers and can't see them. In shared skies they are seen only by the people who are part of that sky.

Where your memories are stored

Photos, titles, descriptions, emotions and albums are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to third parties. Widgets read a reduced copy in a shared container that stays on the device.

iCloud backup

If iCloud backup is on (you can turn it off in Stellare › Backup), Scia saves a copy of your memories in the app's private space on your iCloud Drive, once a day. The backup is tied to your Apple Account and managed by Apple under its privacy policy: we have no access to it. You can also export a backup as a file, which stays wherever you choose.

Shared skies

Shared skies are part of Scia Stellare: both the creator of the sky and those who join need a subscription. A shared sky lives on iCloud, in its creator's storage, and is visible only to its creator and to no more than three invited people: it is never public. The creator chooses people from Contacts with the system picker, without giving Scia access to the address book: Scia receives only the name and the email or number of the chosen person. The email or number is passed to Apple to recognize their Apple Account, and the invitation link is sent with Messages or Mail; the name stays on the inviter's phone until the person accepts. The memories you add (reduced photo, title, text, emotion, date and the signature you choose) are seen by all participants, who can also save or share them. Apple shows participants each other's names according to their Apple Account settings. We have no access to any sky. You can delete your memories from the sky at any time; the creator can delete any memory or the whole sky, and invitees can leave. Copies a participant has saved in their diary remain theirs.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. Scia receives an anonymous identifier from Apple and, only at the first sign-in, your name, to show it in the app and suggest it as your signature in shared skies; it doesn't ask for your email. From the identifier Scia derives an anonymous code that links purchases to your account with Apple. Apple doesn't give apps your Apple Account photo: you choose your profile photo, if you want one. The identifier stays in the device keychain. Name and profile photo are stored on the device and in your iCloud (the app's key-value storage, with an anonymous key), so you'll find them on every iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account: we never see them. Signing out (Stellare › account › Sign out) removes them from the device and they stay in iCloud for when you sign in again; you can remove the photo at any time, also from iCloud. You can remove access in Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple.

Permissions

The camera is used to take a memory, the photo library to add one you've already taken or to choose your profile photo, and the Contacts picker (without access to the address book) to invite someone to a shared sky. Notifications nudge you when you haven't taken photos in a while, at the time you usually save a memory (worked out on the device), and tell you when a time capsule opens and when someone else adds a memory to a shared sky: in that case iCloud only sends the app a signal with no content, and the notification text is put together on the device. Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode are used for the app lock: iOS manages them and Scia receives no biometric data. You can change every permission in Settings.

On-device processing

Photo analysis, text suggestions and the monthly letter happen on the device, with Apple's system features. Your memories are not uploaded for this purpose.

Subscription

Scia Stellare is managed by Apple through the App Store. Scia knows only the subscription status (active, in trial, renewing, with a failed payment) and its dates: it doesn't receive payment details. Refunds and promo codes go through Apple's screens. You can cancel or manage the subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Internet connection

Scia works without a connection and doesn't send data to servers of ours. It uses the internet only through Apple services: the App Store for the subscription, iCloud for backup, shared skies and the account name and photo, and Sign in with Apple.

Sharing

Photos and summaries leave the app only when you choose to share or save them, with system tools, or when you add them to a shared sky.

Deleting your data

A deleted memory stays in «Recently deleted» for 30 days and is then erased. Deleting the app from the device removes all data saved on the phone. The iCloud backup can be deleted in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage › Scia. A shared sky can be deleted (or left) from its menu; its creator can remove a person from «People»; shared memories are deleted one by one. Removing your profile photo from the account page also removes it from iCloud.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be available here. For questions, you can use the contacts listed on the app's App Store page.
